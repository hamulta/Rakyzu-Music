# Rakyzu Music Android 0.4.0

## Scope

This cumulative release completes `0.3.10` and delivers `0.4.0` in one validated artifact, pipeline, tag, and release.

- The final Library gate retains focused unit, lint, Compose instrumentation-compilation, Room, resilience, accessibility, privacy-safe diagnostics, and deterministic 5,000-item projection coverage.
- A dedicated adaptive Create destination creates and lists the authenticated listener's playlists with explicit loading, saved-empty, success, and degraded states.
- Playlist drafts survive recreation through bounded `SavedStateHandle` fields; names and descriptions are normalized and validated on both client and database boundaries.
- Room schema 5 is the account-scoped observable source of truth. Failed or invalid refreshes preserve the last verified collection.
- Supabase playlist metadata uses forced owner-only RLS, explicit grants, anonymous denial, and security-invoker list/create RPCs covered by pgTAP.
- Every playlist starts at revision one, forming the monotonic snapshot boundary for ordered items, reordering, offline conflict recovery, and collaboration in later `0.4.x` milestones.
- Spotify's public playlist, Create, and client-architecture material informed the product principles; all code, UI, schema, content, and branding remain Rakyzu Music originals.
