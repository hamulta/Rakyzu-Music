# Rakyzu Music Android 0.5.20

This cumulative `0.5.16`–`0.5.20` governance release uses versionCode 66.

## Included

- Append-only, reasoned quarantine, takedown, and restore decisions that change listener
  visibility through RLS without destructively deleting catalog metadata.
- Artist and label scoped teams with exact-email assignment, explicit viewer/editor/admin levels,
  and no implicit organization-wide staff authority.
- Exact private WebP artwork inventory, release/artwork review queues, independent approval, and
  immediate or server-time scheduled publication only after every track has standard audio.
- A role-adaptive Android Control Room for enforcement, team scopes, labels, review decisions,
  release schedules, aggregate anomaly status, bounded CSV audit export, and CEO retention intent.
- Worker-side JWT, permission, JSON, UUID, timestamp, media type, payload size, file signature,
  canonical R2 key, RPC, and compensating-delete checks.
- A documented `0.5.21`–`0.5.30` Artist identity train covering exact-email invitations, consent,
  distinct profiles, verification meaning, scoped tools, analytics, commerce, enforcement, and
  deletion governance.

## Role boundaries

- Officer: moderation view and triage.
- Supervisor: Officer capabilities plus final moderation and reversible content enforcement.
- Manager: Supervisor capabilities plus lower-rank staff, drafts, artist/label teams, and audit summary.
- C-Level Executive: Manager capabilities plus media upload, independent review, publication,
  scheduling, and bounded audit export.
- CEO: complete permission set, all-rank control, and non-destructive audit-retention policy.

## Publication contract

Publishing or scheduling a release requires a non-empty track list, standard-quality private media
for every track, uploaded WebP artwork, and approved artwork and release submissions. The account
that submitted a review cannot approve that same submission. A scheduled release is marked ready
atomically but remains absent from listener queries until the database clock reaches its approved
time. Replacing artwork supersedes its previous approval and requires a fresh independent review.
Raw R2 credentials, service-role keys, audit details, and account emails are never returned inside
the Android artifact or audit export.

No Appetize, emulator, ADB, or physical-device execution is claimed for this batch.
