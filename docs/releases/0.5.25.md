## Included

- Optional exact-email Artist link for an existing or future account; versioned in-app terms, consent evidence, active/revoked identity, and a Rakyzu Artist badge. Account email confirmation remains governed by Supabase Auth; no extra Artist-only email verification is required.
- Different animated profile-name palettes for CEO, C-Level Executive, Manager, Supervisor, Officer, and Artist, with a default-style toggle. All accounts can set a WebP-encoded private-R2 profile photo. Active Artists can edit only their linked biography.
- CEO-designated emails: `rakyzudev@gmail.com`, `hamulta215@gmail.com`, `hamultastarius@gmail.com`, `hmultastarius@gmail.com`, and `juevice@gmail.com`, mapped respectively to CEO, C-Level Executive, Manager, Supervisor, and Officer. Matching future signups are assigned by a trusted database trigger.
- Staff catalog controls to select and edit existing or new Artist and album records, with a reversible archive marker and preserved audit/relational history. Artist account email can be added, changed, or cleared.
- Home recommendation card listing, creation, ordering, target-track selection, image replacement, and deletion, including pre-existing seeded cards. Authorized staff can select JPEG, PNG, or WebP images; Android converts them to bounded WebP before upload. Album art uses the same selection flow.
- Audio intake validates MP3, AAC, M4A, WebM, WAV, and FLAC container signatures. The streaming Worker resolves the authoritative media-variant key through a server-checked RPC, rather than serving an obsolete format after replacement.

## Scope and safety

The Rakyzu badge represents a linked profile identity, not endorsement or catalog ownership verification. Artist self-publication, expanded release submissions, analytics, payments, account banning, and account deletion remain future roadmap items. Catalog archive hides content without physically deleting audit history; recommendation-card deletion removes its row and may leave a private R2 object awaiting a separate garbage-collection policy.

Secrets remain in ignored local environment files or managed CI stores. Android receives only the Supabase publishable key and authenticated Worker endpoints; no service-role, Cloudflare, or R2 key is embedded in the APK.
